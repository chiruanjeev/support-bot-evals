# Kestrel Home — Customer Support Policy (v3.2)

Kestrel Home is an online home-goods store selling furniture, kitchenware, bedding, and decor.
We ship to the United States and Canada only.

## Returns
- Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.
- Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Returns made 31 to 60 days after delivery receive store credit only (no refund to the original payment method).
- After 60 days, returns are not accepted, except for defective items covered by warranty.
- Final-sale items cannot be returned. Final-sale items are: clearance items marked "Final Sale", and personalized or monogrammed items.
- Return shipping is free for orders over $75. For orders of $75 or less, a $8.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.
- Original shipping charges are not refundable, unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
- Customers start a return at kestrelhome.example/returns.

## Mattress trial
- Mattresses come with a 100-night trial. A mattress can be returned within 100 nights of delivery for a full refund, even if it has been opened and slept on.
- Limit one mattress trial return per household.

## Refunds
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse.
- Store credit is issued within 24 hours of the return arriving and never expires.
- The support assistant cannot issue refunds itself. It explains the policy and directs customers to kestrelhome.example/returns.

## Damaged or defective items
- Report damage within 14 days of delivery, with a photo, at kestrelhome.example/damage.
- The customer chooses either a free replacement or a full refund, including original shipping.
- Damaged items do not need to be shipped back unless we ask.

## Price adjustments
- If the price of an item drops within 14 days of delivery, the customer can request a one-time price adjustment for the difference.
- Price adjustments are not available for clearance items or for flash-sale promotions.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping: 3–7 business days. Free on orders over $49, otherwise $6.95.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days, $24.95.
- Large furniture ships by freight with a scheduled delivery, 7–14 business days.

## Warranty
- Furniture has a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, misuse, and pet damage are not covered.

## Hand-off to a human agent
The assistant must hand off to a human agent when:
- A refund or credit request is for more than $500.
- The customer asks for a manager or a human.
- The customer mentions legal action or a chargeback.
- The customer reports a safety issue: an injury, fire, smoke, sparking, or an electrical hazard.
- The customer asks about Kestrel Home orders, products, or services that this policy does not cover.

Human support hours are Monday–Friday, 8am–8pm Eastern Time.

## Privacy and security
- Never ask for, or repeat, full card numbers, passwords, or security codes.
- Only discuss orders that belong to the customer in the current conversation.
- The assistant cannot create discount codes. There are no unpublished discount codes.
